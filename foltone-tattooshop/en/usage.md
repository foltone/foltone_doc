# Usage

## Accessing a Tattoo Shop

Tattoo shops are marked on the map with a blip. There are 6 locations across the map. Walk up to a shop and press **E** to enter.

When you enter the shop, your character is automatically stripped to minimal clothing so that tattoos are fully visible on the body.

## Navigating the Menu

The menu features **7 zone tabs** in the navigation bar:

- Head
- Torso
- Hair
- Left Arm
- Right Arm
- Left Leg
- Right Leg

Select a zone to browse the available tattoos for that body area. The camera automatically adjusts to focus on the selected body zone.

## Browsing Tattoos

Each tattoo in the list displays:

- **Owned tattoos**: Shown with an **OWNED** badge and a remove button
- **Available tattoos**: Shown with the price to purchase

Hovering over or selecting a tattoo triggers a **live preview** on your character, allowing you to see exactly how it looks before committing.

## Buying or Removing a Tattoo

Click or press **Enter** on a tattoo to buy or remove it. A **confirmation modal** appears before any transaction is processed, preventing accidental purchases or removals.

## Camera Controls

While in the tattoo shop, you can rotate the camera around your character:

- **A / Q**: Rotate camera left (smooth continuous rotation)
- **D**: Rotate camera right (smooth continuous rotation)

## Keyboard Navigation

The menu fully supports keyboard navigation:

- **Arrow keys**: Spatial navigation through the menu
- **Enter**: Confirm selection
- **Close button**: Exit the shop (original skin and clothing are restored)

## Exiting the Shop

Click the **close button** or use the designated key to leave the tattoo shop. Your original clothing and appearance are automatically restored upon exit.
